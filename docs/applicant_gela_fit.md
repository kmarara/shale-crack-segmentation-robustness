# Applicant-to-GELA fit: evidence map

This document maps the applicant's existing experience to GELA's research methods and problems. It is a positioning guide, not a claim that the applicant already has specialist geomechanics or CT-segmentation expertise.

## 1. Petroleum Chemistry and Technology degree

**Evidence:** Bachelor's degree in Petroleum Chemistry and Technology, University of Zimbabwe.

**GELA relevance:** Subsurface energy systems, fluid-rock interactions, petroleum and natural-gas resource recovery, geological storage and interfacial phenomena.

**How to present it:** Emphasise the combination of chemistry and petroleum-system context. Connect this to understanding fluids, brines, interfaces and porous subsurface environments. Do not present the degree as equivalent to a civil or geotechnical engineering qualification.

## 2. Undergraduate machine-learning dissertation

**Evidence:** Applied AutoGluon and Python, used SHAP for model interpretation, and developed a Streamlit interface.

**GELA relevance:** The laboratory has current work in image-based geomaterial characterisation, physics-informed learning, data-driven meta-modelling and surrogate modelling.

**How to present it:** This is the strongest direct computational bridge. Explain the problem addressed, dataset preparation, validation approach, model comparison, interpretation and what you personally implemented. Highlight reproducibility and critical interpretation, not simply the names of software packages.

**Important boundary:** AutoGluon tabular modelling and SHAP do not by themselves establish experience in deep-learning image segmentation, convolutional neural networks, CT image processing or geomechanical modelling. The public shale project can become evidence of developing those skills once real work has been completed.

## 3. NOIC laboratory experience

**Evidence:** Laboratory and analytical work, with technical documentation.

**GELA relevance:** GELA integrates laboratory experimentation with computational interpretation. Experimental discipline, sample handling, measurement awareness, data quality and documentation are transferable.

**How to present it:** Use concrete examples of analytical methods, quality control, recording procedures, interpretation and reporting. Specify instruments and techniques only where supported by the CV or records.

## 4. Pio Petro internship or industrial exposure

**Evidence:** Internship/exposure at Pio Petro.

**GELA relevance:** Provides an applied petroleum-industry context and awareness of operational constraints and subsurface energy applications.

**How to present it:** State the actual tasks and exposure accurately. Do not inflate observational exposure into independent engineering responsibility.

## 5. Independent H2-brine interfacial-tension investigation

**Evidence:** Independent research interest in underground hydrogen storage and H2-brine interfacial tension, including source checking and a technical clarification request to a paper's author.

**GELA relevance:** The group studies fluid-rock interactions, wettability and interfacial properties in porous media, including geological CO2 storage. Its 2026 surfactant study provides a concrete connection to the applicant's interest in interfacial phenomena.

**How to present it:** Demonstrate scientific care: identify the data source, compare reported values, trace figure-level evidence, distinguish measurement uncertainty from discrepancies, and ask precise technical questions. Make clear that the current investigation is independent study, not a completed experimental research project.

## 6. Proposed public shale CT reproducibility project

**Evidence status:** A public GitHub repository and written research protocol have been initiated. No dataset analysis or model training has yet been completed.

**GELA relevance:** The project is directly inspired by the group's 2026 shale CT segmentation publication and the associated public dataset.

**How to present it now:** Describe it as a self-directed, planned reproducibility and robustness investigation. Do not list model performance, deep-learning implementation or segmentation results until these exist.

**How to present it after completion:** Include exact data audit outputs, specimen-aware split design, baseline implementation, model training details, metrics, error analysis, limitations and repository link. Clearly distinguish work independently implemented from methods adapted from published research.

## Suggested CV emphasis

1. Put the computational dissertation where a research supervisor will notice it, with methods and personal contribution clearly stated.
2. Expand NOIC laboratory experience using concrete analytical and documentation examples.
3. Add the H2-brine investigation as an independent research activity only if the CV has room and the scope is described accurately.
4. Add the shale CT project after tangible milestones exist. Initially list protocol development and literature review; later add data audit, code, experiments and findings as completed.
5. Preserve the existing CV font and layout. Make targeted wording changes rather than redesigning the document.
